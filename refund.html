Cancellation & Refund Policy

This Cancellation & Refund Policy explains the terms applicable to payments made for digital educational services provided by Academiczz Edu.

1. Digital Services

Academiczz Edu provides digital educational tools and services, including admission guidance, college prediction tools, historical admission data and related educational information.

2. Payment

Payments for our services are processed through our authorized payment gateway. The applicable service price will be displayed before payment is completed.

3. Refund Policy

Since our services provide immediate access to digital educational tools and information after successful payment, payments are generally non-refundable once the service has been accessed or unlocked.

4. Failed Transactions

If money is deducted from the customer's account but the payment is not successfully completed or the service is not unlocked, the customer may contact us with the transaction details.

After verification, eligible failed transactions will be reviewed and resolved appropriately.

5. Duplicate Payments

If a customer is charged more than once for the same service due to a duplicate transaction, the duplicate payment will be reviewed and, where applicable, refunded after verification.

6. Cancellation

A service cannot normally be cancelled after the digital service has been successfully unlocked or accessed.

7. Contact for Payment Issues

For payment-related issues, please contact Academiczz Edu with your transaction details.
